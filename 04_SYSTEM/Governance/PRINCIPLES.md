# Olympus Principles

1. **Search before create.** Before creating knowledge, entities, or decisions, check whether the concept already exists.
2. **Memory is not knowledge.** Conversation history and agent memory are evidence or experience, not automatically truth.
3. **Preserve provenance.** Durable knowledge should remain traceable to its sources and, where applicable, its originating Initiative.
4. **Minimum necessary context.** Agents should receive the smallest useful Context Package rather than unrestricted access to everything.
5. **Capability-based routing.** Hermes-agent should route work based on capabilities, not hardcoded assumptions about agent names.
6. **Agents are reusable.** Agents belong to the Pantheon and may serve many Initiatives. Do not create one agent for every Initiative.
7. **Human authority remains explicit.** Important approvals, canonical knowledge changes, destructive actions, external publishing, and other governed actions require the appropriate human approval policy.
8. **Proposed does not mean established.** Insights, decisions, and knowledge may exist in proposed or review states before becoming canonical.
9. **Complexity must earn its place.** Do not create additional agents, schemas, metadata, Skills, or abstractions until repeated real usage demonstrates the need.
10. **Automate last.** Understand and stabilize a workflow before automating it.
11. **One underlying model, multiple experiences.** Future interfaces—Command View, conversational interfaces, dashboards, or spatial/game interfaces—must operate on the same canonical underlying system. The future Olympus game experience visualizes the real system; it is not a separate game database.
