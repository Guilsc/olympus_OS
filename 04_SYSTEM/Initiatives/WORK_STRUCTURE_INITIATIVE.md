# WORK STRUCTURE INITIATIVE

**Status:** READY FOR ZEUS INTAKE  
**Target branch:** `feature/apollo-agent-work`

## Goal

We want Olympus to establish a clear, reusable operating structure for **WORK**.

The goal is to make WORK capable of receiving professional initiatives, understanding what outcome is expected, deciding who should own the initiative, coordinating the capabilities required to deliver it, and maintaining continuity across future WORK initiatives.

This structure should support professional work such as:

- projects and deliverables;
- business analysis;
- product, process, and solution work;
- professional research;
- reports and presentations;
- professional sites and applications;
- professional content and visual deliverables;
- project and delivery coordination;
- other professional outcomes that clearly belong to WORK.

The structure should be useful beyond a single task. It should create durable ownership and an operating model for WORK without adding unnecessary complexity.

## Requested outcome

Treat this as a new Olympus Initiative.

Use the current Olympus context, governance, architecture, principles, existing Realms, existing ownership model, current capabilities, and any relevant attached or repository material to determine:

1. what WORK needs in order to operate effectively;
2. who should own this Initiative and the resulting WORK operating responsibility;
3. whether an appropriate owner already exists;
4. if no appropriate owner exists, what should be created or established to provide durable ownership;
5. what capabilities, collaboration, context, memory, permissions, lifecycle, quality controls, and operating boundaries are required for that owner or structure;
6. how this Initiative should move from design into an operational, validated state.

Do not assume the implementation shape in advance. Use Olympus governance and current context to decide the correct path.

If an existing owner or capability is sufficient, use it.

If no suitable owner exists, establish the necessary owner using the current Olympus standards and relevant canonical material.

## Working approach

Start with Zeus' normal Initiative intake and first-pass review.

Zeus should understand the request, goal, boundaries, and any governance implications, then continue through the normal Olympus operating model.

Use Hermes-agent, existing Realm Owners, Skills, Tools, and other Olympus mechanisms according to their established responsibilities.

Do not create extra review gates or stop only to produce a review document.

Let the normal flow expose gaps or ambiguities.

If something is unclear and the answer materially affects the outcome, Zeus may ask the Human Owner for clarification.

If an issue appears during execution:

- identify the actual issue;
- handle it at the correct Olympus layer;
- preserve provenance;
- distinguish Initiative-specific decisions from reusable system lessons;
- improve the reusable process only when the lesson genuinely applies beyond this Initiative;
- continue from the appropriate point rather than restarting the entire flow.

Do not silently work around governance or technical constraints.

## Success condition

The Initiative is successful when WORK has a clear, operational ownership model that can accept and deliver future professional initiatives under Olympus governance.

The resulting structure should have:

- clear accountability;
- clear boundaries with other Realms;
- an intentional operating lifecycle;
- the capabilities required for WORK;
- appropriate collaboration with other Olympus owners;
- correct context, memory, and knowledge behavior;
- verified permissions and runtime behavior where applicable;
- a validated path from intake to delivery;
- enough operational identity and continuity to own WORK outcomes over time.

The final form should emerge from Olympus' existing architecture and the execution of this Initiative, not from assumptions embedded in this request.
